# HSE guidance

Contains public sector information published by the Health and Safety Executive and licensed under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

These PDFs are HSE's, not this project's, and are not covered by the repository's licence. The HSE logo and any third-party images inside them are not covered by the Open Government Licence. Each file is the unmodified download named in `data/corpus/register.yaml`, where its SHA-256 is pinned; `uv run gigawhat data fetch-hse` downloads and checks them again.
