## 1 Purpose

This procedure controls access to Harrowmere Electricity substations, the issue of operational keys and the escorting of visitors, so that unauthorised people are kept away from danger and the Control Engineer always knows who is on site.

## 2 Scope

This procedure applies to ESS-RVL, ESS-ASH, ESS-KGM, ESS-BRK and ESS-ORC, and to the key cabinet at the Ashford Road depot (DEP-ASH). Safety lock keys are covered by PR-ELEC-012. Entry to the Kingsmead cable tunnel CT-KGM-01 is covered by PR-ELEC-031.

## 4 Definitions

Operational key: a restricted-profile key that opens substation gates and buildings.

Key register: the record, held by the Electricity Safety Manager, of every operational key and its holder.

Visitor: anyone without an authorisation under the Distribution Safety Rules, including unauthorised contractors and inspectors.

Escort: an Authorised Person (HV) or Competent Person (HV) who accompanies visitors.

## 5 Responsibilities

Electricity Safety Manager: keeps the key register, approves key issue and audits keys each year.

Senior Authorised Person (HV): approves visits to ESS-RVL.

Authorised Person (HV): approves visits to other sites and may act as escort.

Competent Person (HV): may act as escort.

Control Engineer: records arrivals and departures and follows up anyone overdue.

## 6 Procedure

### 6.1 Issuing and controlling keys

6.1.1 Issue operational keys only to Authorised Persons (HV) and Competent Persons (HV) with a current authorisation, with the approval of the Electricity Safety Manager.

6.1.2 Record each key's serial number, the holder's authorisation number and the date in the key register, and have the holder sign for it.

6.1.3 Keep your key on your person or locked in your vehicle key safe, and never lend it.

6.1.4 Do not copy keys. Replacements are ordered only by the Electricity Safety Manager.

6.1.5 Return your key when you leave the company, change role or your authorisation lapses.

6.1.6 Sign out contractor day keys from the DEP-ASH key cabinet and return them the same day.

### 6.2 Arriving at and leaving a site

6.2.1 On arrival, tell the Control Engineer the site ID, how many people are present, the purpose of the visit and the expected duration.

6.2.2 Sign the site log.

6.2.3 Lock the gate behind you unless it is attended for vehicle access.

6.2.4 If working alone, follow PR-CORP-004 and agree a check-in interval of no more than 60 minutes with the Control Engineer.

6.2.5 Before leaving, check all doors and gates are locked, sign out of the site log and tell the Control Engineer.

6.2.6 As Control Engineer, if someone has not signed out within 30 minutes of their expected time, call them. If there is no answer, start the escalation in PR-CORP-004.

### 6.3 Visitors

WARNING: Visitors have no training in HV hazards and may approach exposed conductors or enter danger areas without realising it.

6.3.1 Arrange visits in advance with an Authorised Person (HV). Visits to ESS-RVL also need Senior Authorised Person (HV) approval.

6.3.2 Before entry, check each visitor's identity and brief them on hazards, restrictions and emergency arrangements.

6.3.3 Provide a hard hat, safety footwear, high-visibility clothing and eye protection.

6.3.4 Escort visitors at all times inside the perimeter, with no more than four visitors per escort.

6.3.5 Do not let visitors touch or operate equipment or enter transformer compounds.

6.3.6 Do not carry out switching while visitors are in the switchroom.

6.3.7 Do not admit anyone under 16, and record all visitors in the site log.

### 6.4 Contractors

6.4.1 Contractors with a Harrowmere authorisation follow 6.1 and 6.2.

6.4.2 Treat contractors without an authorisation as visitors. They must be escorted or work in an area segregated from HV equipment, as agreed and recorded by an Authorised Person (HV).

6.4.3 Brief contractors on the site hazards and emergency arrangements before work starts.

### 6.5 Lost keys and security breaches

6.5.1 Report a lost or stolen operational key to the Control Engineer and the Electricity Safety Manager as soon as you notice, and within 24 hours at most.

6.5.2 As Electricity Safety Manager, decide whether locks at the affected sites must be changed.

6.5.3 Report signs of intrusion or an unlocked gate to the Control Engineer. Do not enter if intruders may be present, and call 999.

6.5.4 Report security breaches under PR-CORP-001.

### 6.6 Emergency

6.6.1 Call 999 for any injury or fire, then inform the Control Engineer.

6.6.2 As escort, lead visitors to the assembly point in the site emergency information and account for everyone.

6.6.3 Ask members of the public who report a power cut or damaged power lines to call 105.
