## 1 Purpose

This procedure sets out how to excavate safely near live Harrowmere gas mains, so that mains are found before they are struck and are protected while exposed.

## 2 Scope

This procedure applies to all excavation within 5 m of a live LP, MP or IP main by Harrowmere staff and contractors, including bar holing and trial holes. It applies to excavations near MAIN-IP-01, MAIN-MP-03, MAIN-LP-17 and MAIN-LP-22, and to all other mains on the network.

## 4 Definitions

Cable avoidance tool (CAT): A hand-held locator that detects buried metallic services by the signals they carry.

Signal generator: A transmitter used with the CAT to put a traceable signal on a metallic main or tracer wire.

Hand-dig zone: The area around a main where only hand tools may be used.

Trial hole: A hole dug by hand to expose a main and confirm its position and depth.

## 5 Responsibilities

Head of Gas Network Operations: Makes sure excavation teams are trained in this procedure and in the use of the CAT.

Authorised Person (Gas): Issues permits for mechanical excavation within 3 m of MAIN-IP-01 and advises on work near cast iron mains.

Excavation supervisor (a Competent Person (Pressure Systems) or contractor supervisor): Obtains plans, locates and marks mains, and controls the excavation.

Gas Network Controller: Receives reports of any damage to a main.

## 6 Procedure

### 6.1 Plans and planning

6.1.1 Obtain Harrowmere gas mains plans, and plans from other utilities, dated no more than 30 days before the start of work.

6.1.2 Treat plans as a guide only. Mains may not be where the plans show, and depths are not guaranteed.

6.1.3 Mark the expected line of every main on site with yellow paint before digging.

6.1.4 For any work within 3 m of MAIN-IP-01, obtain a permit-to-work under PR-GAS-021 before any mechanical excavation.

### 6.2 Locating mains with the CAT

CAUTION: Polyethylene mains such as MAIN-MP-03 and MAIN-LP-17 cannot be found with a CAT unless a tracer wire was laid with them. Absence of a signal does not mean there is no main.

6.2.1 Check the CAT and signal generator are within calibration and pass their self-test.

6.2.2 Sweep the whole work area in power mode and radio mode, then in signal generator mode.

6.2.3 Use the signal generator on metallic mains (MAIN-IP-01 steel, MAIN-LP-22 cast iron) and on any tracer wire.

6.2.4 Mark every located service on the surface and compare it with the plans.

6.2.5 Re-scan the excavation every 300 mm of depth as digging progresses.

### 6.3 Trial holes and hand-dig zones

WARNING: Striking a live main with an excavator can release gas at high pressure and cause fire, explosion and serious injury. Hand digging near mains is mandatory.

6.3.1 Dig trial holes by hand to expose and confirm the position and depth of each main before any mechanical excavation within the work area.

6.3.2 Hand dig within 0.5 m either side of the confirmed line of any LP or MP main.

6.3.3 Hand dig within 1.5 m either side of the confirmed line of MAIN-IP-01, and keep mechanical excavators at least 3 m away unless the permit allows otherwise.

6.3.4 Use spades and shovels with careful, angled strokes. Do not use picks, forks, pins or bars to break ground over a main.

6.3.5 Do not use breakers within 0.5 m of any main.

### 6.4 Working near exposed mains

6.4.1 Support exposed PE and steel mains at intervals of no more than 1.5 m, and never use a main as a step or lifting point.

6.4.2 Do not undermine more than 1 m of cast iron main, such as MAIN-LP-22, without support and the advice of the Authorised Person (Gas). Cast iron fractures easily.

6.4.3 Keep heavy plant and spoil at least 1 m back from the edge of an excavation containing a main.

6.4.4 Keep these minimum clearances when laying other plant near a gas main: 300 mm parallel and 150 mm crossing for LP and MP mains; 600 mm parallel and 300 mm crossing for MAIN-IP-01.

6.4.5 Backfill with at least 150 mm of fine fill free of stones around the main, and lay marker tape 300 mm above it.

### 6.5 Damage to a main

6.5.1 Report any damage to a main to the Gas Network Controller, even if no gas is escaping, including scrapes to the coating of MAIN-IP-01 and scores on PE mains.

6.5.2 Do not backfill a damaged main until it has been inspected. PE mains with scores deeper than 10% of the wall thickness must be repaired.

6.5.3 Report every strike or near miss under PR-CORP-001.

### 6.6 Emergency

6.6.1 If gas is escaping, stop all engines, do not try to stop the gas, and get everyone out of the excavation and upwind.

6.6.2 Keep the public and vehicles at least 25 m away, or 50 m for MAIN-IP-01, and keep ignition sources away.

6.6.3 Report the escape to the Gas Network Controller for action under PR-GAS-003, or call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.

6.6.4 Call 999 for fire or injury.

## 7 Records

The excavation supervisor keeps the plans used, the CAT sweep record and trial hole depths with the job record. Damage reports are logged in the work management system and kept for the life of the main.
