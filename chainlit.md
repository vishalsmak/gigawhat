# GigaWhat

An assistant for operational staff at Harrowmere Energy, a **fictional** UK gas and electricity utility. It finds the approved procedure, summarises the evidence in operational records with citations, and suggests next steps. People make the decisions.

- **Pick a persona** from the menu at the top. What you can see depends on the role.
- **Safety-critical questions** (isolation, switching, purging, confined spaces) get word-for-word procedure extracts that an Authorised Person must release. Switch to the Authorised Person persona to release or decline them.
- **Emergencies** get emergency numbers straight away.
- Every question and answer is written to an append-only audit trail. The Auditor persona shows yours.

This is a demo with invented data. Do not use it for real operational work.
